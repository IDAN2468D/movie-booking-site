# Movie Site Analytics KPI Definitions

## Core Financial Metrics
- `Gross Revenue`: Total order value including 18% VAT.
- `Net Revenue`: `Gross Revenue / 1.18`.
- `VAT Amount`: `Gross Revenue - Net Revenue`.
- `AOV (Average Order Value)`: Total Gross Revenue / Completed Orders.

## Customer Retention
- `Retention Rate`: (Repeat Customers / Total Unique Customers) * 100%.
